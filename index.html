        How is this private?
        1. Your browser turns the password into a SHA-1 hash, e.g. 5BAA61E4C9B93F3F…
        2. Only the first 5 characters of that hash are sent.
        3. The service replies with every leaked hash starting with those 5 characters (hundreds of them).
        4. This page checks the list for a match locally. Neither the password nor its full hash ever leaves your browser.

        Privacy: everything runs in your browser. Nothing is logged, stored or sent, except the optional 5-character hash prefix above. Estimates are approximate; use a password manager.
